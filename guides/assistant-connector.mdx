The TinyCloud connector lets an AI assistant work with your personal records:
todos, goals, reading lists and other things you keep track of. The records
live in your own TinyCloud space. The assistant can read, add, change, move,
delete and search them, and only after you approve each kind of access.

This page covers the connector that assistants list in their connector
directories. It has five tools and works only with personal records. For
secrets, files, key-value data or SQL databases, use the
[full TinyCloud MCP server](/cli/mcp) or the CLI instead.

## Before you start

You need:

- An assistant that lists TinyCloud in its connector directory.
- A TinyCloud account. If you do not have one, you create it during sign-in
  with an email address, a passkey or Google.

You do not need to install anything or paste a key.

## Connect

1. Open your assistant's connector directory, find **TinyCloud** and choose
   **Connect**. In Claude, the directory is under **Customize**, then
   **Connectors**.
2. Sign in with OpenKey, TinyCloud's sign-in service. OpenKey shows the name of
   the assistant that is asking. Choose **Allow**.
3. Ask the assistant to connect TinyCloud. The first time, it shows you an
   approval link.
4. Open the link, read what it asks for, and choose **Approve**.
5. Go back to the assistant and tell it to continue.

Signing in does not give the assistant access to your records. Step 4 connects
it to your account, and each app's records need their own approval later.

Each assistant uses its own address. Connecting TinyCloud in two assistants
gives you two separate connections, each with its own approvals.

| Assistant | Address |
| --- | --- |
| Claude | `https://mcp.tinycloud.xyz/claude/mcp` |
| ChatGPT | `https://mcp.tinycloud.xyz/chatgpt/mcp` |
| Grok | `https://mcp.tinycloud.xyz/grok/mcp` |
| Muse | `https://mcp.tinycloud.xyz/muse/mcp` |

Whether TinyCloud appears in a directory is decided by that assistant. You
only need an address if your assistant asks you to enter one.

## Approvals

The connector asks before it can do anything new. A tool that needs access
returns a link instead of a result, and the assistant shows it to you.

| Approval | When it is asked for | What it allows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Connection | The first time you connect | Lets the connector find your TinyCloud account and its apps |
| An app's records | The first time the assistant works with an app, such as todos | Reading that app's records, or reading and changing them |
| Searchable copy | The first time you ask a question across many records of an app | Keeping and querying a searchable copy of that app's records |

- An approval link works for 15 minutes and only once.
- An approval lasts 30 days. After that the connector asks again.
- If you decline, nothing is read or written, and the assistant stops that
  request.
- The approval page says that OpenKey cannot confirm who sent the request.
  Approve only a link that your own assistant just showed you.

## What you can ask

- "Add a todo called Review release checklist, then show it to me."
- "What is on my todo list?"
- "Mark Review release checklist as done."
- "Delete the todo called Old draft."
- "Use a searchable copy to count the todos I finished this month."

The first time you save a record, the assistant asks for your timezone, for
example `Europe/London`, so that records carry your local date and time. It
does not need your city or location.

## Tools

| Tool | What it does | Changes data |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `tinycloud_connect` | Checks the connection and returns an approval link when one is needed | No |
| `tinycloud_apps_context` | Returns your apps, your local time, your saved timezone and the rules for handling records. Saves your timezone when the assistant passes one | Only your timezone setting |
| `tinycloud_apps_read` | Lists the records in a folder and reads up to 100 records | No |
| `tinycloud_apps_write` | Creates, replaces, moves or deletes up to 10 records in one call | Yes |
| `tinycloud_apps_query` | Refreshes the searchable copy of one app, then runs one read-only query over it | Only the searchable copy |

Your assistant may ask you to confirm a tool before it runs. That confirmation
is the assistant's own; it does not replace an approval in OpenKey.

## How records are kept

- An **app** is a group of records, such as todos. Each app has a short
  description of its record kinds, which the assistant creates the first time
  and reads before it writes.
- A record has a key, a version, a time and your data. The connector adds who
  wrote it.
- Before changing a record, the assistant reads it. If the record changed in
  the meantime, TinyCloud refuses the change and the assistant reads it again.
  It never overwrites a newer version silently.
- Deleting a record removes it from lists and reads. The storage node may keep
  the bytes of earlier versions.

## Searchable copy

Reading records one folder at a time is slow for questions such as "how many
did I finish this month?". For those, the connector can keep a searchable copy
of one app's records.

- It is optional and needs its own approval.
- It is a copy. Your records stay the source of truth, and a query never
  changes them.
- A query is one `SELECT` or `WITH` statement in SQLite syntax over a table
  named `entries`, with the columns `key`, `kind`, `folder`, `id`, `day`, `v`,
  `at`, `by`, `data` and `rec`.
- The first query for an app builds the copy and can take several seconds.

## What the connector cannot do

- Read or write secrets, files or any data outside personal records.
- Run SQL against your other databases, or read arbitrary keys.
- Share or publish anything.
- Make payments or generate images, audio or video.

## Privacy

<Warning>
The connector and your assistant both see the records they work with. The
connector receives each tool call and its result in plain text, and your
assistant may keep what it was shown. Read
[Security and trust](/concepts/security-and-trust) before you connect.
</Warning>

- Do not keep passwords, API keys, payment-card numbers, government
  identifiers or protected health information in personal records.
- The connector's logs hold the tool name, the outcome and the duration. They
  do not hold keys, values or queries.
- Disconnecting the connector in your assistant does not delete your records.
- The [privacy policy](https://tinycloud.xyz/privacy) describes how TinyCloud
  handles your account and content.

## Remove access

- **In your assistant:** disconnect or remove the TinyCloud connector.
- **In OpenKey:** sign in at [openkey.so](https://openkey.so), open
  **Connected apps**, then **Manage app access**, and remove the assistant.
  Its access ends within about a minute.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "TinyCloud approval is required" with a link | The connector needs an approval | Open the link, approve, then tell the assistant to continue |
| "This approval link is unavailable" | The link expired or was already used | Ask the assistant to connect TinyCloud again |
| `AUTH_REQUIRED` or `SESSION_NOT_FOUND` | The connection lapsed | Open the new link in the answer and approve |
| `INPUT_INVALID` | The assistant sent something the tool does not accept. The message names the field | Ask the assistant to correct it and try again |
| `TIMEZONE_REQUIRED` | No timezone is saved yet | Tell the assistant your timezone |
| `CHANGED` | The record changed since it was read | Ask the assistant to read it again, then repeat the change |
| `COPY_PARTIAL` | The searchable copy is not complete yet, so the answer is not either | Ask the same question again |
| `STORAGE_QUOTA_EXCEEDED` | Your TinyCloud storage is full. Nothing was saved | Free up space at [account.tinycloud.xyz/storage](https://account.tinycloud.xyz/storage) |

For anything else, write to [team@tinycloud.xyz](mailto:team@tinycloud.xyz) or
see [tinycloud.xyz/support](https://tinycloud.xyz/support). Say which
assistant you used and what happened. Do not send passwords, codes or approval
links.
